## Anti-Patterns (Article-Specific)

- ❌ Full-width article body (max must be 65ch for readability)
- ❌ Body font < 18px on desktop
- ❌ H1 font-weight 800/900 (over-spec, look unstable)
- ❌ Inline emoji in heading (use SVG icon or styled `<span>`)
- ❌ Missing `alt` text on hero image
- ❌ Heading skip (h1 → h3)
- ❌ Color-only emphasis (always combine with bold or icon)
- ❌ Anchor `#` without href (use `<a href="#id">` with anchor target)
- ❌ Sticky sidebar overlapping content on scroll (use proper `top: 100px` offset for sticky header)
- ❌ "Back to top" button overlapping floating bottom nav (mobile)
